# Free local watchlist

No payment, login, email or server-side collection. Selections stored only in browser localStorage under ttc-watchlist-v1, capped at 100, and may be cleared by the browser. Not an automatic alert service. Users can search official tax IDs or names and navigate to government profiles. Browser storage can be blocked; selections then persist only in memory. Browser interaction and assistive technology verification pending. No creditor-related personal information is collected.
