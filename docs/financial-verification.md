# Independent financial verification

## Pilot scope
Copperas Cove, Coryell County, Copperas Cove ISD. Confirm exact legal entity and official identifiers before matching records.

## Evidence requirements
For each metric preserve agency, original document URL, retrieval timestamp, reporting period, original filename and SHA-256, page/table or row/column locator, units, reporting scope, extracted value, transformation formula, published value, difference, reviewer and review date. Archive original bytes where permitted.

## Status
Pending: original source not inspected. Source verified: original bytes and locator checked. Reconciled: independent calculation agrees with the published value under documented scope. Discrepancy: unresolved difference. Unavailable: source or metric unavailable; never substitute zero. Modeled: estimate with assumptions, not a reported balance.

## Priority
1. FY2025 local debt principal, interest and repayment for each pilot entity.
2. 2021–2025 adopted property tax rates and levies.
3. Audited financial statements, adopted budgets and debt schedules; distinguish fiscal periods, tax years, cash and accrual bases.
4. Reconcile statewide publication differences only after matching scope, dates and revisions.

## Release gates
No verification claim without original evidence. Preserve unresolved differences and prior versions. Require independent calculation and regression tests before publishing revised values. Do not merge until owner approves. Production remains unchanged during this phase.
