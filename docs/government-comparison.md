# Government comparison v2

Free bilingual comparison of up to three government profiles. Annual tax rates 2021–2025 are dollars per $100 taxable value. FY2025 reported debt principal, remaining interest, repayment, and reported population appear separately. Debt principal per resident = FY2025 reported principal / FY2025 reported population when population > 0; it is not an individual debt obligation. Five-year historical principal bars use local-debt-history.json annual reports and do not imply real-time changes. Missing values are not zero. No cross-government aggregation or inferred household tax bill. No login, payments or tracking. Browser interaction and mobile assistive technology testing remain pending.
