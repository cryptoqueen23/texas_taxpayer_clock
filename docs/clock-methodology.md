# Historical tax clock

The simulation starts at zero on page load and multiplies elapsed active simulation seconds by the 2023 annual reported tax total divided by 31,536,000. Pause and reset are available. Reduced-motion preference disables automatic start. This is an illustration, not actual receipts, current-year revenue or outstanding debt. Debt snapshots are static. Remaining interest and repayment are withheld until source-level schedules are verified.
